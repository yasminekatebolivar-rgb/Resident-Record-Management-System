System Overview

Modernizing Resident Data Management

The Barangay Resident Record Management System (RRMS) is a digital platform that simplifies the recording, organization, and retrieval of resident and household information — replacing manual forms and spreadsheets with a centralized database.

🗄️

Centralized Database

All resident information in one secure location

🧾

Paperless System

No more manual forms and scattered spreadsheets

🔒

Admin-Only Access

Secure access restricted to authorized personnel
